Frequently asked questions

What is a good European alternative to HubSpot?

Options from European companies include Brevo, Teamleader, Odoo and SuperOffice CRM. Brevo is the closest for email marketing plus CRM. Teamleader and Odoo add quotes and invoicing.

Where are these companies based?

Brevo: Germany; Teamleader: Belgium; Odoo: Belgium; SuperOffice CRM: Norway. SuperOffice CRM (Norway) is outside the EU and marked on this page.

Does a European company mean my data stays in Europe?

Not automatically. A company based in Europe must follow European data protection law, but it may still host data in other regions or use other providers. Check where your data is stored and the provider's list of sub-processors before you switch.
