Frequently asked questions

What is a good European alternative to Google Drive?

Options from European companies include Nextcloud, Internxt, Filen, Koofr, pCloud and Tresorit. Nextcloud adds online office documents, which is the closest match to Google Drive with Docs and Sheets.

Where are these companies based?

Nextcloud: Germany; Internxt: Spain; Filen: Germany; Koofr: Slovenia; pCloud: Switzerland; Tresorit: Switzerland. pCloud (Switzerland) and Tresorit (Switzerland) are outside the EU and marked on this page.

Does a European company mean my data stays in Europe?

Not automatically. A company based in Europe must follow European data protection law, but it may still host data in other regions or use other providers. Check where your data is stored and the provider's list of sub-processors before you switch.
