Frequently asked questions

What is a good European alternative to AWS?

Options from European companies include Hetzner, OVHcloud, Scaleway and IONOS Cloud. None of these offer every AWS service. Compare the specific services you use, and pick an EU region.

Where are these companies based?

Hetzner: Germany; OVHcloud: France; Scaleway: France; IONOS Cloud: Germany.

Does a European company mean my data stays in Europe?

Not automatically. A company based in Europe must follow European data protection law, but it may still host data in other regions or use other providers. Check where your data is stored and the provider's list of sub-processors before you switch.
