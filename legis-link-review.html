What is Legis-Link?

Legis-Link is an AI-powered construction compliance reference tool built for tradespeople, foremen, and project managers who need accurate code answers on site — without carrying the standard, calling the office, or waiting for an engineer.

You open a browser on your phone, pick your trade and region, type a compliance question in plain English, and get the exact standard reference back in seconds. There is nothing to install, no account required for the free tier, and it works on any phone.

1. Compliance check (Free)

Compliance check
FREE
Why it exists

Most compliance questions on site are binary: is this compliant or not? The compliance check tool answers that question with a clear status, the specific code clause, and any caveats — in under 10 seconds.

How to use it

Select your trade and region. Type the question as you would ask a colleague. The more specific you are, the more precise the answer.

Q: "Is 2.5mm² TPS cable compliant for a 20A circuit in a wall cavity NSW?"
● COMPLIANT — 2.5mm² copper TPS is compliant for a 20A circuit in a standard wall cavity installation, provided ambient temperature does not exceed 40°C and cables are not grouped with more than 2 other circuits without derating. For grouped or high-ambient installations, verify against derating tables.
Ref: AS/NZS 3008.1.1:2017 Table 4; AS/NZS 3000:2018 Clause 3.5
Key benefit

Prevents costly rework from wrong material selection. A single rework typically costs more than a year of Pro subscription.

2. Technical calculations (Pro)

Technical calculations
PRO
Why it exists

Cable sizing, pipe sizing, voltage drop, and heat load calculations require multiple variables. Getting one wrong means either over-specifying (cost blowout) or under-specifying (rework or safety risk). This tool does the calculation and shows the working.

How to use it
Q: "Cable size for 45A load, 30m run, 240V single phase, clipped direct NSW"
● COMPLIANT — 10mm² copper conductor required.
Working: 45A at 30m on 240V single phase gives VD = 1.97% — within 3% sub-circuit limit. Clipped direct installation, 40°C max ambient, no derating required.
Ref: AS/NZS 3008.1.1:2017 Table C1; AS/NZS 3000:2018 Clause 3.6.2
Key benefit

Replaces spreadsheet lookups and manual table reading. Works for electrical (AU/UK/US/CA), plumbing, and HVAC calculations with correct region-specific tables applied automatically.

3. RAMS generator (Pro)

RAMS generator
PRO
Why it exists

A Risk Assessment and Method Statement used to take a foreman half a day to produce. On commercial sites, no RAMS means no work. This tool generates a complete, printable RAMS in 60 seconds from a task description.

How to use it

Describe the task, trade, and region. The output includes: Section 1 — Hazard Register with risk ratings and control measures; Section 2 — numbered Method Statement; Section 3 — required qualifications and certifications. All with specific regulation citations.

Key benefit

A qualified foreman producing a RAMS manually takes 2-4 hours. This tool produces a draft in under 2 minutes. The foreman reviews, adjusts site-specific details, and signs off. The time saving alone justifies the Pro subscription.

4. Daily toolbox talks (Free)

Daily toolbox talks
FREE
Why it exists

Every site should run a 5-minute safety briefing before work starts. In practice, most don't — because producing them takes time and most foremen don't have a template library. Legis-Link provides a different pre-start briefing for every trade, every day.

How to use it

Tap the Toolbox button in the app. The daily topic for your selected trade appears with: hazard register, pre-start checklist with tick boxes, discussion question for crew engagement, and regulatory reference. Pro users can download a printable PDF with crew sign-off register.

Key benefit

Builds a daily compliance habit that keeps the free tier valuable even between queries. 70 topics across 10 trades rotating daily — no repeated content for weeks.

5. Visual compliance — photo upload (Pro)

Visual compliance
PRO
Why it exists

Sometimes you're looking at an installation and you don't know exactly what to ask. This tool lets you photograph the work and ask "is this compliant?" The AI analyses the image and gives a structured assessment of what it can and cannot determine from the photo.

How to use it

Tap the cam button in the app. Select a photo from your camera roll or take one. Ask your question or leave the placeholder. The AI returns: what it can see, compliance assessment, specific issues with standard references, what it cannot assess from the photo, and a recommendation.

Key benefit

No other construction compliance tool on the market accepts site photos for compliance assessment. This is currently a unique capability. Results are clearly marked as preliminary visual checks — not certified inspections.
