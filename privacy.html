Privacy notice

Last updated 03 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data eutech.directory collects, why, and your rights. Contact: hello@eutech.directory.

Browsing the directory

We do not use cookies and do not track you across sites. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or collect personal identifiers; it records page views, the referring site, and the browser and country of the visit in aggregate.

Submitting a tool

When you submit a tool, the form sends what you type (tool name, website, category, country, description, what it replaces and, if you give it, your email) to Formspree, which delivers it to us. We use it only to review the listing and, if you gave an email, to contact you about it. We publish the tool details, never your email. Submissions are kept until reviewed, then deleted or kept with the listing record (without your email if you ask).

Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to its use. Email hello@eutech.directory and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

We update this page when what we collect changes, and show the date at the top.
